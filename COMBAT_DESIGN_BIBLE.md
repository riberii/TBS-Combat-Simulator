# COMBAT DESIGN BIBLE — CHAINS OF THE ABYSSAL CHOIR
**Version:** 3.0 (October 2026)  
**Lead Combat Designer & Systems Architect:** Principal Game Architect  

---

## 1. Core Combat Philosophy

1. **Strategic Intent Over Blind Attrition:** Every enemy action is telegraphed with intent label, target indicator, and estimated threat. Players make informed choices to attack, guard, reposition, interrupt, or deploy defensive barriers.
2. **Deterministic Rules & Transparent RNG:** Combat calculations (hit, crit, defense mitigation, status chances) are fully deterministic for a given run seed. Probability odds are visible on tooltips and HUD.
3. **Deep Class Identity & Asymmetry:** No two classes share the same resource loop or tactical purpose. Party composition creates synergistic combat chains rather than repeating identical actions.

## 2. Signature Mechanic: The Choir & Counterpoint System

### A. Tactical Ability Tags
Every hero ability carries one or more tactical tags that define its role in the Choir:
- **`SETUP`**: Applies marks, traps, or conditions that prime enemies for follow-up strikes.
- **`STRIKE`**: Direct physical or martial pressure targeting health or posture.
- **`WARD`**: Defensive barriers, damage mitigation, intercept shields, and party shelter.
- **`SHIFT`**: Repositioning, decoy diversion, evasion, and threat manipulation.
- **`SILENCE`**: Spell disruption, channel breaking, cooldown delaying, and debuffing.
- **`DETONATE`**: Consumes primed status stacks, marks, or terrain hazards for massive explosive payoffs.

### B. Echoes and Counterpoints
- When a hero executes an ability, they leave an **Echo** on the target or battlefield for 2 rounds.
- When a different party member targets an entity with an active Echo using a complementary tag, a **Counterpoint** is triggered:
  - `SETUP` + `STRIKE` → **Resonant Rupture**: Deals +25% bonus damage and applies Vulnerable.
  - `WARD` + `SHIFT` → **Sanctuary Veil**: Grants a temporary barrier (+15 HP) and sheds threat.
  - `SETUP` + `DETONATE` → **Harmonic Blast**: Consumes the Echo to deal area damage to adjacent targets.
  - `SILENCE` + `STRIKE` → **Staggering Cadence**: Interrupts telegraphed enemy actions and reduces target speed.
- Counterpoint triggers award **Choir Resonance**, which charges team ultimate abilities.

### C. Boss Overtures
Bosses do not simply attack; they conduct **Overtures**—multi-turn musical rituals telegraphed on the timeline:
- **Measure 1 (Preparation):** The boss channels power, displaying a glowing telegraphed area or ritual seal.
- **Measure 2 (Execution):** The catastrophic attack unleashes unless countered.
- **Counterplay:** Players can disrupt the Overture by triggering a specific Counterpoint (e.g. `SILENCE` + `STRIKE`), breaking an environmental anchor, or sheltering in safe zones.

## 3. Class Kits & Resource Loops

### 1. Warrior (Momentum)
- **Resource:** Momentum (0–100), generated by dealing damage, guarding, and breaking armor.
- **Loop:** Build Momentum via `Cleaving Arc` and `Challenge`; spend it on devastating finishers like `Execution`.
- **Passives:** Iron Resolve (+10 HP), Keen Edge (+8% ATK), Momentum Master (+1 Rage per strike).

### 2. Tank (Bastion / Resolve)
- **Resource:** Stamina & Shield Bastion.
- **Loop:** Maintain `Aegis Guard` on allies; taunt high-threat foes; build Resolve to counter-slam with `Shield Breaker`.
- **Passives:** Thick Plates (+12 HP), Shield Mastery (+10% DEF), Vigilance (+5 Stamina/turn).

### 3. Rogue (Combo & Advantage)
- **Resource:** Combo Points (0–5, expandable to 6).
- **Loop:** Build Combo via `Quick Strike` and `Shadowstep`; spend on `Eviscerate` or `Smoke Bomb` escape.
- **Passives:** Shadow Step (+8 Stamina), Lethal Precision (+10% Crit), Combo Mastery (+1 max Combo).

### 4. Witch (Mana & Sigils)
- **Resource:** Mana (0–100).
- **Loop:** Cast delayed `Rift Sigil` and `Chain Spark`; manage mana regeneration and cooldowns.
- **Passives:** Deep Wellspring (+15 Mana), Arcane Surge (+8% Spell Power), Ether Affinity (+3 Mana/turn).

### 5. Fallen Angel (Lucidity & Restraint Chains)
- **Resource:** Lucidity (0–100) & Restraints (0–6 active curses).
- **Loop:** Battle while bound by Abyssal Chains. Use `Writhe` and `Break Restraint` to shatter bindings while managing HP and Lucidity backlash.
- **Passives:** Martyr's Endurance (+12 HP), Grace Touched (+8 Lucidity start), Iron Will (+8% break chance).

### 6. Kitsune (Spirit & Foxfire)
- **Resource:** Spirit (0–100, expandable to 110).
- **Loop:** Generate Spirit through evasions and `Foxfire Thread`; summon `Paper Moon Double` decoys to absorb enemy intent.
- **Passives:** Spirit Affinity (+10 Spirit), Fox Fire Focus (+8% Foxfire DMG), Trickster Mark (+1 turn Decoy).

### 7. Vampire (Blood & Shadows)
- **Resource:** Blood (0–100).
- **Loop:** Extract Blood via `Sanguine Lunge`; enter `Veil of Night` to evade attacks and heal.
- **Passives:** Dark Vitality (+10 HP), Blood Thirst (+8% Blood gain), Veil of Mist (+5 HP absorption).

### 8. Dragonkin (Heat & Overdrive)
- **Resource:** Heat (0–100, threshold 100, expandable to 110).
- **Loop:** Build Heat through martial pressure and `Scale Guard`; vent heat through `Ember Breath` and `Wingbeat`.
- **Passives:** Furnace Heart (+10 HP), Scale Temper (+8% DEF), Controlled Burn (+10 Heat cap).

## 4. Companion System (Active Passives)

1. **Ashen Raven:** Grants +20% gold on victory.
2. **Spectral Wisp:** Regenerates +5 Mana and +5 Stamina for all living heroes at the start of each round.
3. **Grave Hound Pup:** Increases party attack power by +10% across all strikes.
4. **Obsidian Scarab:** Hardens party defenses by +3 flat damage mitigation.
5. **Silver Sprite:** Restores 5 HP to the living hero with the lowest HP percentage at the end of each round.

## 5. Regional Economy & Merchant Matrix

Item prices scale dynamically based on dungeon depth and merchant region:
- **Oath Road (Halls 1–3):** Base price × 0.95 (Pilgrim discount).
- **Cathedral of Unheard Hymns (Halls 4–6):** Base price × 1.25.
- **Thorned Court & Ember Sepulcher (Halls 7–9):** Base price × 1.40.
- **The Abyssal Rift & Choir's Heart (Halls 10–12):** Base price × 1.60 (Deep scarcity surcharge).

**Class Affinities:**
- Rogues receive 15% discount on tools, smoke bombs, and lockpicks.
- Witches receive 10% discount on mana draughts and arcane scrolls.
- Warriors & Tanks receive 10% discount on armor kits and sharpening stones.
- Dragonkin face a surcharge on anti-draconic wards in Cathedral regions.
